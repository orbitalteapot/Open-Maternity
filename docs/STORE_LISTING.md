# Store listing and store forms

Texts for Google Play and the App Store in all four languages, plus the answers for the privacy and health forms. Character limits: Play title 30, short description 80, full description 4000. App Store name 30, subtitle 30, keywords 100.

The Norwegian, Swedish and Danish texts should be read by a native speaker before publishing.

## English

**Title:** Open Maternity: Pregnancy Kit
**Subtitle (App Store):** Calm, private pregnancy tools
**Short description (Play):** Contraction timer, kick counter and checklists. Free, offline, no ads.

**Full description:**

Open Maternity is a calm toolkit for your pregnancy. It is free, open source and has no ads.

TOOLS
• Contraction timer: tap when a contraction starts and stops. See how long they last, how far apart they are and how many came in the last hour. A dim night theme turns on after 10 pm.
• Movement counter: count your baby's movements in a session.
• Hospital bag: a checklist you can add your own items to.
• Appointments: keep your check-ups and the questions you want to ask in one place.
• Medicines: a daily reminder for vitamins or medicines, and a tap to mark them as taken.
• Weight: log your weight when you want to.
• History and a PDF summary you can share with your midwife.

YOUR NUMBERS, ONE TAP AWAY
The app has no phone numbers built in. Add your maternity ward, midwife and others once, and call them with one tap from the timers.

PRIVATE AND OFFLINE
No account, no tracking, no analytics. Everything stays on your phone and works without internet. Export a backup file whenever you like.

IN YOUR LANGUAGE
English, Norwegian, Swedish and Danish.

Open Maternity helps you keep track. It does not give medical advice and does not replace your midwife, doctor or maternity ward.

**Keywords (App Store):** pregnancy,contractions,contraction timer,kick counter,labour,midwife,hospital bag,baby,maternity

## Google Play forms

**Category:** Medical, or Health & Fitness (Health & Fitness is the safer choice for a tracking tool).
**Contains ads:** No. **In-app purchases:** No.
**Target audience:** 18 and over.
**Privacy policy URL:** the published `docs/PRIVACY.md` (for example https://github.com/orbitalteapot/open-maternity/blob/main/docs/PRIVACY.md; the repo must be public).

**Data safety**
- Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types? **No.**
- Leave every data type unticked. The app has no network access and sends nothing.
- Data deletion: users can delete all data in the app (Settings → Delete everything).

**Health apps declaration** (App content → Health apps)
- Tick the features the app really has: pregnancy and reproductive-health tracking, and medication reminders. The exact category names in the form may change, so pick the closest ones.
- The app is **not** a medical device: it does not diagnose, treat, monitor vital signs or tell the user what to do. It records what the user logs and shows averages.

**Content rating (IARC):** no violence, sex, drugs, gambling or user interaction. Expect the lowest rating.

## App Store Connect forms

**Category:** Health & Fitness (secondary: Medical).
**App Privacy:** **Data Not Collected.**
**Age rating:** answer No to everything, except *Medical/Treatment Information*: **Infrequent/Mild** (the app mentions pregnancy and medicines).
**Export compliance:** no encryption (already set in Info.plist with `ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption = NO`).
**Review notes for Apple:** "Open Maternity is an offline pregnancy toolkit. It records what the user logs (contractions, movements, checklists) and shows simple averages. It gives no medical advice or alerts; the contraction timer always offers a button to call the user's own maternity ward, using a number the user entered. No account is needed. To try it with data: open Settings and add a due date; time two or three contractions."
