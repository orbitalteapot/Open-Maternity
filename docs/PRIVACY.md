# Privacy policy: Open Maternity

*Last updated: 29 October 2026*

Open Maternity is a free, open-source pregnancy toolkit. It was built so that nothing you log ever leaves your phone unless you choose to share it yourself.

## What the app collects

**Nothing.** We do not collect, receive, store or share any personal data. The app has:

- no account and no sign-in,
- no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking of any kind,
- no servers. On Android, store builds do not even have permission to use the internet.

## What stays on your phone

What you enter is saved only in the app's private storage on your phone: contractions, movement sessions, your hospital-bag list, appointments, medicines and when you took them, weight entries, your due date, your language and theme, and the phone numbers you add yourself. The app has no phone numbers built in and never looks any up.

On Android, the app is excluded from Google's automatic cloud backup. On iPhone, the app's data is part of your phone's own backup (iCloud or computer), under your Apple account. We never have access to it.

## When something leaves your phone

Only when you do it yourself:

- **Export everything:** saves a backup file where you choose.
- **Summary for your midwife:** makes a PDF and opens your phone's share menu. You choose who receives it.
- **Call:** opens your phone's dialler with the number you saved. The call itself is handled by your phone.

## Reminders

Medicine reminders are scheduled by your phone's own notification system. If you allow notifications, the reminder text (for example "Time for Vitamin D") may show on your lock screen, depending on your phone's settings.

## Deleting your data

Settings → **Delete everything** removes all logs, lists and numbers from the app at once. Uninstalling the app also removes everything.

## Children

The app is meant for adults.

## Medical disclaimer

The app helps you keep track. It does not give medical advice and does not replace your midwife, doctor or maternity ward.

## Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date. Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/orbitalteapot/open-maternity/issues.

## Norsk (bokmål)

Open Maternity samler ikke inn noe. Appen har ingen konto, ingen analyse, ingen reklame og ingen sporing, og vi har ingen servere. Alt du logger, lagres bare på telefonen din. Noe forlater telefonen bare når du selv eksporterer en sikkerhetskopi, deler en PDF-oppsummering eller ringer et nummer du har lagret. Innstillinger → Slett alt fjerner alle data. Appen gir ikke medisinske råd og erstatter ikke jordmor, lege eller fødeavdeling.
